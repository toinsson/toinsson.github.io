I am trained as both a researcher and a software engineer, specialising in user experience (UX) and machine learning (ML/AI). I bring advanced coding skills and strong team management capabilities to my work.

I earned a PhD [[1](https://theses.gla.ac.uk/78981/)] in Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), where my research focused on developing innovative solutions for complex challenges in novel gestural interactions. This involved co-designing prototypes based on user requirements, capturing critical interaction data, building datasets for deep probabilistic modeling, conducting UX experiments, and analysing results. My findings have been consistently presented and published in peer-reviewed journals and conferences [[2](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0272509), [3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.14788)].

In addition to my academic background, I have extensive experience in software development across various environments, including R&D, large corporations, and startups. I have delivered production-ready software written in C for massively parallel embedded devices and designed, developed, and deployed machine learning frameworks for document classification using large language models (LLMs). I prioritise performance, leverage modern technologies, and focus on minimizing technical debt. Where possible, I have open-sourced my projects [[4](https://github.com/toinsson/pyrealsense), [5](https://github.com/toinsson/pysdtw)].

Finally, my experience also extends to team management and mentoring. I frequently take on the role of SCRUM master, leading daily stand-ups, planning sessions, and retrospectives. Additionally, I have served as a subject-matter expert, reviewing conference proceedings and tutoring university courses in AI and ML. I am passionate about fostering high performance within teams, with a keen focus on the growth and contributions of each team member.
